# Bud Light Boycott: 2023 AB InBev US Revenue — Pre-Resolution Intelligence Dossier

**Question:** Will AB InBev report a year-over-year decline in US revenue in their Q2 2023 earnings report due to the Bud Light boycott?
**Market Opened:** April 2023 (Following the Dylan Mulvaney controversy)
**Information Cutoff:** July 26, 2023 (Eve of AB InBev Q2 2023 earnings release)

---

## 1.0 Executive Summary & Resolution Criteria

**Summary:** In April 2023, a sponsored social media partnership between Bud Light and transgender activist Dylan Mulvaney — involving a custom commemorative can — triggered one of the most rapid and commercially devastating consumer boycott campaigns in US corporate history. The controversy, amplified across right-wing social media and conservative political networks, drove a historic collapse in Bud Light's US sales that transformed America's best-selling beer into a cautionary tale about brand alignment in the culture war era. This event measures whether a purely socially-driven consumer movement (not driven by product quality, price, or legal action) produced a measurable, financially certified impact within one fiscal quarter.

**Resolution Contract:** This market resolves "YES" if Anheuser-Busch InBev's (AB InBev, NYSE: BUD) official Q2 2023 earnings report (released July 27, 2023) discloses a year-over-year percentage decline in US revenue (defined as US net revenue or US volume sales compared to Q2 2022). It resolves "NO" if the US revenue figure is flat or shows any year-over-year increase.
- *Measurement Standard:* Resolution is based strictly on the official AB InBev earnings press release and investor presentation. Segment-specific US figures are used; global revenue performance is irrelevant to this contract.

## 2.0 Key Social & Commercial Indicators (Snapshot as of Cutoff)

*Note: This event is a pure measurement of social-movement commercial impact. The outcome is a direct function of consumer behavior, not economic cycles or supply factors.*
- **Bud Light US Volume Decline (Nielsen retail scanner data, May 2023):** Down approximately 24–26% year-over-year in weekly sales, the largest documented single-brand decline in US beer market history during a non-recall event.
- **Total Brand Value Loss Estimate (BrandFinance):** ~$395 million in brand equity destroyed between April and June 2023.
- **Replacement Beneficiaries:** Modelo Especial overtook Bud Light as the #1 selling beer in the US by dollar sales in May 2023 — an event that had never occurred in over two decades.
- **Social Media Campaign Scale:** #BoycottBudLight trended nationally for 15+ consecutive days across Twitter/X, Facebook, and TikTok.
- **Conservative Political Amplification:** Kid Rock posted a viral video shooting Bud Light cans with a rifle. Travis Tritt banned Bud Light from his tour rider. US Senate Republicans called for an investigation. The cultural boycott became embedded in MAGA political identity.

## 3.0 Factual Reconstruction (Timeline of Social Escalation)

- **April 1, 2023:** Dylan Mulvaney posts an Instagram video showing a custom Bud Light can bearing her likeness, part of a paid brand partnership celebrating her one-year anniversary of gender transition. The post is standard micro-influencer marketing.
- **April 3–5, 2023:** Right-wing media figures (Tucker Carlson, Ben Shapiro, Daily Wire) feature the partnership extensively. The story transitions from niche culture-war commentary to mainstream conservative outrage.
- **April 5, 2023:** Kid Rock posts a viral video opening fire on cases of Bud Light with a rifle, receiving 10+ million views. The boycott becomes a viral social media challenge.
- **April 14, 2023:** AB InBev CEO Michel Doukeris issues a tepid, non-committal public statement refusing to explicitly disavow or support the Mulvaney partnership. The ambiguity alienates both sides — conservatives see it as insufficient repudiation, while LGBTQ+ advocates see it as abandonment of a partner.
- **April–May 2023:** Weekly Bud Light sales continue declining at an accelerating rate. The brand falls from #1 to #3 in US beer sales by volume. Internal AB InBev marketing executives are placed on "leave of absence." No major pro-Bud Light counter-campaign materializes from celebrities.
- **May 2023:** Modelo Especial (also owned by a different company — Constellation Brands — in the US) becomes the #1 beer by dollar sales. Bud Light had held this position uncontested since 2001.
- **June 2023:** Bud Light launches patriotic summer marketing campaign ("Easy to Drink, Easy to Enjoy") entirely devoid of social justice messaging. The campaign fails to recover sales velocity. Conservative commentators mock it as "hiding."
- **July 26, 2023:** AB InBev executives brief analysts in pre-earnings calls. Market consensus expects a significant US volume decline in Q2.

## 4.0 Structural Mechanics of the Social Boycott

**The "Hot-Button Trigger" Mechanism:**
Unlike economic boycotts (which require sustained organizational effort), this was a "disgust-based" social trigger embedded in pre-existing cultural grievances around corporate DEI practices, transgender visibility in public life, and the perceived imposition of progressive values by major brands.
- *The Tipping Point:* The Kid Rock video transformed political outrage into a visible, easily replicable social action (pouring out beer, shooting cans). It required no organizational structure — the act itself was the protest.

**The "Middle Brand" Vulnerability:**
Bud Light's specific market position made it uniquely vulnerable. Unlike niche craft beers (immune to culture war dynamics) or prestige spirits (purchased by wealthier consumers less price-sensitive to political identity), Bud Light is a working-class, blue-collar staple predominantly purchased by the exact demographic most mobilized by conservative media.

**The Replacement Behavior:**
Unlike many boycott movements where boycotters simply reduce consumption, Bud Light boycotters visibly and publicly switched to a competitor (Coors Light, Miller Lite, Modelo). This replacement behavior was social — posting on social media about "My New Beer" — which sustained the boycott's momentum.

## 5.0 Divergence: Will the Boycott Show in Financials?

**The Bull Case for YES (Revenue Decline):**
Weekly Nielsen retail scanner data shows 24–26% declines sustained for 12+ weeks. Even accounting for on-premise (bar/restaurant) recovery and international offsets, the US segment should reflect a significant decline.

**The Bull Case for NO (Revenue Holds):**
AB InBev's US operations include non-Bud Light brands (Michelob Ultra, Stella Artois). If these brands see offsetting gains from consumers who remain AB InBev customers but switch within the portfolio, segment-level US revenue could be partially protected. However, Bud Light represented approximately 25% of all AB InBev US volume — its collapse would need extraordinary offsetting growth to avoid a segment decline.

## 6.0 Key Unknowns & Catalyst Triggers

1. **AB InBev's Segment Reporting Granularity:** Does AB InBev break out US-specific revenue clearly enough in their earnings release for this contract to resolve without ambiguity? Some companies report regional revenue opaquely.
2. **The AB InBev Portfolio Offset:** Did Michelob Ultra or Stella Artois see a meaningful domestic US revenue surge sufficient to offset Bud Light's losses within the same reporting segment?
3. **International Performance vs. US Segment:** AB InBev's overall global revenue may be positive even if the US segment is declining. The resolution depends strictly on the US-specific figure.

## 7.0 Scenario Projections

**Scenario A: The Social Boycott is Financially Real (Resolves YES — Base Case)**
- **Thesis:** 12+ weeks of 24–26% volume declines in the largest-revenue segment of AB InBev's portfolio cannot be absorbed by any brand mix offset. The Q2 2023 US revenue figure shows a clear year-over-year decline.
- **Projected Outcome:** AB InBev discloses US volume declining ~14–17% for Q2 year-over-year, with US revenue declining in the high single-digit percentage range. The boycott is confirmed as a financially material event.

**Scenario B: Offset Recovery Holds Revenue Flat (Resolves NO — Tail Risk)**
- **Thesis:** Michelob Ultra's growth, Stella Artois's premium positioning gains, and on-premise recovery in bars/restaurants partially offset Bud Light's retail collapse.
- **Projected Outcome:** AB InBev reports flat or slightly positive US revenue, attributing volume declines to Bud Light's temporary disruption while touting overall portfolio resilience.
